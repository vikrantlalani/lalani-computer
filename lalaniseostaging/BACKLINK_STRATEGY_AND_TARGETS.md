# Backlink Strategy & Targets

## 1) Strategy Overview
The off-page SEO strategy for the next 3–6 months will focus on building high-trust, highly relevant backlinks that establish Lalani Computers as a leading enterprise IT hardware supplier and infrastructure expert in Mumbai. The approach prioritizes securing "low-hanging fruit" such as official vendor/partner listings and localized B2B citations. Once foundational trust is established, the strategy will shift toward earning editorial features in Indian IT publications and leveraging existing client relationships for case-study mentions. This methodical approach will safely improve domain authority, organic rankings, and AI-search visibility.

## 3) Concrete Target Ideas

- **Vendor/Partner Listings**
  - *Example Targets:* Dell Partner Portal, HP Reseller Locator, Cisco Partner Network.
  - *Target Page:* Homepage (`/`) or specific brand pages.
  - *Suggested Anchor:* "Lalani Computers - Authorized Partner" or naked URL.

- **Local Business Citations**
  - *Example Targets:* JustDial, IndiaMart, TradeIndia, Sulekha, local Mumbai business registries.
  - *Target Page:* Homepage (`/`).
  - *Suggested Anchor:* "Lalani Computers", "Lalani Computers Mumbai".

- **Industry Articles & Features**
  - *Example Targets:* CRN India, ChannelWorld India, regional B2B tech blogs.
  - *Target Page:* Solutions pages (e.g., `/solutions/data-center` or `/solutions/networking-wifi`).
  - *Suggested Anchor:* "enterprise data center setups", "corporate IT infrastructure in Mumbai".

- **Case-Study Mentions**
  - *Example Targets:* Websites of existing clients (e.g., Kores India, Navnit Motors).
  - *Target Page:* Relevant solution page or homepage.
  - *Suggested Anchor:* "IT infrastructure managed by Lalani Computers", "Lalani Computers".

## 4) Anchor Text Policy
To maintain a natural and safe backlink profile, the outreach team should aim for the following anchor text mix:
- **~50% Branded:** "Lalani Computers", "Lalani Computers Mumbai", "Lalani".
- **~30% Brand + Service/Keyword:** "Lalani Computers data center solutions", "Lalani Computers IT hardware".
- **~20% Topical / Naked URLs:** "enterprise IT hardware supplier in Mumbai", "turnkey IT setup", "https://lalanicomputers.com".

## 6) Outreach Workflow
- **Prospecting & Research:** The marketing team must manually identify the correct contact persons (e.g., partner managers for vendors, editors for tech publications, procurement officers for clients). Tools like LinkedIn or Hunter.io can help find specific email addresses.
- **Tracking:** Use a shared Google Sheet or CRM to track domains, contact info, outreach dates, status, and responses.
- **Cadence & Follow-ups:**
  - Day 1: Send initial personalized email.
  - Day 5: Send first follow-up (brief nudge).
  - Day 12: Send second and final follow-up.
  - If no response, mark as "Unresponsive" and prioritize other targets.
