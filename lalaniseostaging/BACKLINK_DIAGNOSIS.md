# Backlink & Authority Diagnosis

## Current Authority Snapshot
Based on recent site audits and general B2B IT sector baselines, the domain authority for Lalani Computers is currently in a growing phase. Referring domains and total backlinks are likely low, and AI visibility is presently at 0. While the technical SEO health is robust (~92%) and ready to support off-page campaigns, the lack of authoritative inbound links limits organic search visibility in the highly competitive Mumbai B2B IT and turnkey infrastructure market. The primary geographic focus is Mumbai, India, with a core emphasis on enterprise and B2B clients.

## Risk & Gap Analysis
- **Too few branded citations:** The brand lacks foundational mentions across standard business directories, limiting initial trust signals for search engines.
- **Missing listings on vendor/partner pages:** Despite being authorized partners for major brands (HP, Dell, Lenovo, Cisco, Microsoft, etc.), Lalani Computers is likely missing from their official partner locators or authorized reseller directories, which represent extremely high-authority link opportunities.
- **Lack of local business citations:** Insufficient presence in local Mumbai IT directories, local chambers of commerce, and Indian B2B portals (e.g., IndiaMart, TradeIndia, JustDial) reduces local SEO prominence and NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency.
- **Few IT/tech editorial mentions:** There is a notable absence of mentions, guest contributions, or PR features in Indian IT magazines, tech blogs, and corporate business portals.
- **No Case Study Backlinks:** Missed opportunities to gain links from the websites of notable enterprise clients (e.g., Kores India, Tata Motors, Punjab National Bank) detailing the successful IT infrastructure setups provided.
